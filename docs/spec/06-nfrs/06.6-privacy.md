## §6.6 Privacy & Compliance Requirements

### NFR-PR01 — Data Minimization
Store only what is necessary:
- avoid collecting unnecessary PII
- do not store secrets in logs
- do not store raw OAuth tokens beyond what’s needed for identity

### NFR-PR02 — Data Retention
- Soft-deleted entities retained for grace period:
  - tasks: 30 days
  - documents: 30 days
  - workspaces: 90 days
  - users: 90 days (or immediate hard delete if required)
- Audit logs retained for 365 days (configurable)

### NFR-PR03 — GDPR/FERPA Readiness (v1-level)
- Provide:
  - data export for user (v2 recommended)
  - account deletion (soft-delete) support
- Limit access by roles (supervisor view limited to their workspace content)
