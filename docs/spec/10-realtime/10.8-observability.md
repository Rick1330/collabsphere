## §10.8 Observability (Realtime)

### Metrics
- `socket.connections.active` (gauge)
- `socket.events.emitted.count` (counter by event)
- `socket.join_denied.count`
- `socket.broadcast_latency_ms` (histogram)
- `collab.connections.active` (gauge)
- `collab.persist_failures.count`

### Logs
- connection accepted/rejected
- join accepted/denied (workspaceId)
- permission denied on write events
- persist failures with requestId correlation
